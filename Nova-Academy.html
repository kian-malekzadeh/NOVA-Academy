Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is there an age limit for learning English at NOVA Academy?

No. Our courses are designed for all ages from 4 years old and up. Each age group receives content tailored to their needs and learning style.

2. Are classes online or in-person?

All classes are online and interactive. You only need high-speed internet and a device (mobile, tablet, or laptop).

3. How is my language level determined?

After registration, you take a free online placement test (including listening, speaking, and writing skills) so you start exactly where you are.

4. Can I choose my own teacher?

Yes, you can choose from native and bilingual teachers who best match your learning style and goals.

5. What if I'm not satisfied with the course?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied for any reason, the full amount is refunded.
